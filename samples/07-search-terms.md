# Derivatives and the Mean Value Theorem

A derivative measures how fast a function changes. Where a limit describes a
value a function approaches, a derivative describes the slope of its tangent line.

## Short terms

The natural logarithm, written ln, is the inverse of the exponential function.
Its derivative is the reciprocal function. In Leibniz notation the symbol dx
names a small change in the variable, and a derivative is a ratio of two such
changes.

## The mean value theorem

The mean value theorem says that a smooth curve between two points has at
least one point where the tangent is parallel to the chord joining them. It is
the standard tool for showing that a function with a zero derivative is constant.

## Words used for the partial-match test

Derivatives of polynomials follow the power rule. A chord is a straight segment
between two points of a curve.
